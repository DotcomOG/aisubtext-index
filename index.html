Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of ensuring that AI systems — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini — accurately and favorably represent your brand when buyers ask questions about your category, competitors, or company. While SEO focuses on ranking in Google search results, AEO focuses on how AI synthesizes and presents information about you. As AI becomes the first stop in the buyer research journey, AEO is becoming as critical as SEO for B2B companies.
AIsubtext is built exclusively for AEO. We measure your current AI brand score across four major AI engines, identify exactly where and how AI gets your brand wrong, and provide a prioritized content roadmap to improve how AI represents you. Unlike SEO tools that optimize for keyword rankings, AIsubtext optimizes for AI answer accuracy — the new battleground for brand visibility.
AI brand visibility refers to how accurately and favorably AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude describe your company when buyers ask about your category, your brand, or your competitors. A high score means AI gives accurate, complete, and positive descriptions of your brand. A low score means AI is getting you wrong, omitting you entirely, or misrepresenting your position.
We generate a set of queries that mirror real buyer research — questions about your category, your use cases, and direct brand searches. We submit these to multiple AI engines and evaluate the responses on accuracy, completeness, competitive positioning, and tone. The result is a score from 0 to 100, benchmarked against your industry peers.
AI systems learn from publicly available content — your website, press coverage, third-party reviews, documentation, and more. Our content recommendations focus on creating and optimizing content in the formats AI systems ingest most effectively: structured data, clear positioning statements, authoritative third-party mentions, and consistent terminology across all your digital touchpoints.
Our Starter plan monitors ChatGPT and Perplexity — the two most commonly used AI tools for B2B research. Growth and Agency plans add Claude and Gemini. We continuously evaluate new AI engines and add them to the platform as adoption grows.
SEO optimizes for search engine rankings — getting your pages to appear in Google results. AI brand optimization (AEO) ensures that when AI systems synthesize information about your brand, they do so accurately and favorably. The underlying content strategies overlap, but the signals, formats, and evaluation criteria are fundamentally different. You need both. AIsubtext is built for AEO.
The free audit includes your AI Brand Score across 2 engines, a summary of how AI currently describes your brand, identification of your top 3 visibility gaps, and a prioritized list of quick wins to improve your score. It's a complete diagnostic — no strings attached. Paid plans add monitoring, deeper analysis, and correction support.
